# How to Identify Which AI Model You're Speaking To

## Quick Answer

You can identify the AI model you're interacting with through several methods:

### 1. **Check the Platform Interface**
- Most AI platforms display the model name/version in the UI
- Look for model selectors or dropdowns in the interface
- Check settings or account information sections

### 2. **Ask the Model Directly**
You can ask questions like:
- "What model are you?"
- "Which version are you running?"
- "What are your capabilities?"

*Note: Model self-identification may vary depending on platform policies.*
