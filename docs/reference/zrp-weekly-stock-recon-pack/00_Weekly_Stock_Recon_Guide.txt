 | 1. Purpose
This pack lets you run the weekly stock recon investigation yourself, in your own Claude. You upload the week's PumpIT reports and get back the same master book that head office produced for 1-21 September. It gives a diagnosed reason for every recon line, the shift-level evidence, the standing controls, a live site scorecard and the control actions.
Everything is built from PumpIT reports. The weekly Google Sheet (PumpIt Stock Recon Problem Items) is no longer used: its line list, balancing screen and last stock take dates are rebuilt from the same PumpIT data, so nothing is drawn out, pasted into a sheet and imported again.
Ownership is as follows:
Operations (you and your Business Manager) owns the outcome: the Monday presentation, the actions, discipline and training.
Ruan is the investigator. He pulls the reports, runs the evidence and chases the sites under your direction. He supports the Monday meeting with evidence; he does not present in your place.
Site managers explain every line and close it with a finding, within 48 hours.
Finance tests storeroom overs against supplier statements, and no stock take over or reversal is posted without a reason.

 | 3. One-time set-up (about 15 minutes)
3.1 Enable code execution and skills
The skill runs code, so code execution must be on. On ZRP's Team or Enterprise plan an organisation owner enables it under Organization settings > Plugins & skills: "Code execution and file creation", and "Skills". On an individual plan it is under Settings > Capabilities. If the Skills page is missing, ask IT to enable these two settings.
3.2 Upload the skill
In Claude, open Customize > Skills.
Click "+", then "+ Create skill", then "Upload a skill".
Select zrp-stock-recon-weekly.zip. Do not unzip it first.
Make sure the skill is switched on.
3.3 Create the project
Open Projects and create a new project named "Stock Recon - Convenience".
Open the project's instructions and paste in the full text of Project_Instructions.txt.
Add Required_Reports.md and summary_14Sep-20Sep2026.json to the project knowledge.
Each week, add that week's summary file to the project knowledge. Claude then compares each week with the one before.

6. Running the week
Open the Stock Recon - Convenience project and start a new chat.
Attach all the report files in one message.
Type: "Run the weekly stock recon for the week of 21 to 27 September. Give me the master book and the brief for the Business Managers."
Claude checks that every report is there, runs the analysis (1-3 minutes) and returns the book, a summary file and the brief.
Read the data notes first. Any site-day held back as incomplete, or any site with no recon lines in the week, is a data problem to fix before the findings are sent.
Download the summary file and add it to the project knowledge (section 3.3).
Send the book and the brief to your Business Managers and site managers, with the due dates.

Status: every line starts Open. Close a line only with a written finding: what actually happened, not an opinion. "GRV 16334 captured 90 instead of 100" is a finding; "counting error" is not. Anything not answered in 24 hours is set to "Escalated to Ops Exec" that day.
8. The rules
Recon variance = POS - (opening + issued - closing). Negative is short.
Storeroom = site stock take - recon. A negative storeroom is a loss outside the recon (issue not written up, or the key holder). A positive storeroom is not a loss: it is an invoice, GRV or credit problem.
The opening must equal the previous closing. Where it does not, amend the OPENING; never change a closing to match.
In balancing, an over may reduce a short, but an over may never become a short, a short may never grow, and the latest closing count stands.
An over within 10% of a whole pack is an uncaptured issue.
Steers potatoes and buns are issued in full on the day of delivery: issue = GRV.
Recipes already include yield. A shortfall is never peel or trim.
The same item short at three or more sites points to a recipe, pack or link fault, not theft.
Each site is analysed only up to its last complete recon day.
No cause is stated without evidence. A consistent short is not a "wrong count" until a recount proves it.
9. The Monday meeting
You and your Business Manager present. Use the book's Site Scorecard and Control Actions tabs. Cover:
For each site: lines to investigate, closed, still open, and the value at risk. Every site below 100% closed explains why.
The outstanding-items register: every line still open from earlier weeks, with its owner and new date.
Openings below the previous closing, and balancing breaches: explained, reversed, and who was accountable.
Outcomes: staff charged, key holders held accountable, invoices and GRVs corrected, and the training given.
Control actions: done, late, and what is needed from IT or Finance.
10. When something goes wrong
What you see
 | What to do
 | Claude says a report is missing
 | Export it and attach it in the same chat. Check that the file name has not been changed.
 | A site is held back for more than one day
 | Balancing or the POS posting is incomplete. The site manager completes it; re-export the Stock Recon Detail and re-run.
 | A site has no lines for the week
 | The Stock Recon Detail was exported before the site balanced, or with the wrong date range. Re-export and re-run.
 | Not-counted lines look too high
 | Check that report 3 (last stock take date) was uploaded and run for the week under review. Report 2 leaves out counts with no variance.
 | Claude states a cause without evidence
 | Challenge it: ask for the GRV, the count or the shift that proves it. If there is none, the next step is a question to the site.
 | The skill does not appear
 | Code execution or skills is not enabled for your account (section 3.1).
