AGORA · Zululand Retail & Petroleum · BUILD PLAN · 3 SEPTEMBER 2026

One system for the whole estate, built on the database you already trust

Agora is the successor to PumpIT: the same PumpIT database underneath, a new application on top, organised around the working day rather than the software's menus. This page says what it is, how it will be built, in what order, and what we need from you.

In one screen

the whole plan
What Agora is PumpIT rebuilt as a web application — desktop and phone — with four sections: Today, Trade, Control, Setup All Group Operations, Reconciliation & Analysis. The dip must tie to the pump, the Z-read to the cashup, the declaration to the bank.
What stays The PumpIT database, every record in it, and the overnight imports into MIST_Import Nothing is re-keyed and nothing moves. Agora adds its own tables and procedures alongside the existing ones and reads the same imports PumpIT reads.
What changes The screens, the reports, the day-close flow, the approvals, the reconciliation desk and the exception register 161 legacy reports become 97 named for the question they answer; every branch function becomes a step in one guided day.
How it is delivered Three stages, each usable on its own, with PumpIT running beside Agora until the numbers agree for a full month Foundation → the branch day and head-office control → trading, reporting and cutover. Branch capture is switched off in PumpIT first, reporting last.

Why the shape changes

organise by the day, not the module

PumpIT is organised the way it was written: Branch Functions, Head Office Functions, two kinds of masters, a Reporter and Business Tools. Nobody has ever thought "I need a Branch Function". Agora has four sections, named for the work.

TODAYWhat must happen nowThe branch day as one guided close: POS files, pump readings, fuel input, Z-reads, stock areas, banking, drop safe. Head office sees the same list live.
TRADEHow the business is doingThe group position, the site league, the weekly Exco pack, budgets and the report library — with scope as a setting, not a separate report.
CONTROLWhat does not reconcileThe exception register, the reconciliation desk, deductions and approvals. Every open item has an owner, an age and a rand value.
SETUPThe rules the rest obeysBranches, profit centres, category margin bands, expense codes, fuel matrix, people, users and access, thresholds.

Four working rules run through every screen. The system proposes, the person confirms: Z-read allocation, bank matching and expense coding arrive with evidence and a confidence, and are auto-accepted above a threshold you set. An exception is an object with a life: raised, owned, actioned, cleared — it does not vanish when a report is closed. Write once, flow everywhere: a till short reaches the cashup, the deduction schedule and the payroll file from a single capture. Every number carries its provenance: click a figure and see which overnight load it came from and which corrections were applied.

How it is built

the same database, a new application

Agora is a Laravel web application connected directly to the PumpIT SQL Server database. The business rules live in the database as stored procedures, where they are fast, auditable and close to the data; the application handles screens, access and workflow. The other systems — the POS imports in MIST_Import, the reporting extracts in Alteryx, the wet-stock sheet — are read, never written.

One branch key everywhereBranchId leads every table, every procedure and every screenThe same branch numbering PumpIT and the imports already use. A user only ever sees the branches they are granted.
Rules in the databaseStored procedures own the numbers and the writesFast on 170 GB of history, auditable in one place, and the same answer whether a screen, a report or a schedule asks.
Forward-only changesEvery schema change is a numbered, versioned stepNothing is ever dropped in the first release; the legacy tables are wrapped, not rewritten, until a screen is rebuilt on them.
Reference data runs onceA seed ledger records what has been loaded, at which versionRoles, permissions, menus, categories and templates cannot be accidentally reloaded over live edits.
Built from componentsOne KPI card, one grid, one chart, one menu — reused on every screenA fix to the grid fixes every grid. The look is Agora's own: light and dark, the same on every screen.
Phones from day oneCapture screens are designed for a phone; wide screens get a purpose-built phone viewPump readings, fuel input, drop safe, waste, staff shorts and purchase requests can be raised from the forecourt.

The order of work

three stages

Each stage is usable on its own and is finished before the next starts. The first customer-visible screen is the branch console at the start of stage two; everything in stage one is the ground it stands on.

STAGE 1Platform foundationThe application, connected to a copy of PumpIT; users migrated with role → permission access; the theme, menus, search and components; email and templates; every master under Setup. What you see: sign-in, Setup, the shell.
STAGE 2Today & ControlThe branch day and the head-office desk: day close, fuel, Z-reads, cashups, drop safe, shorts and deductions, imports and reconciliation, stock counts and production, purchase approvals, utilities, assets, the exception register, overnight-load monitoring. What you see: PumpIT's branch and head-office functions, replaced.
STAGE 3Trade, reporting & cutoverThe report library and engine, trading dashboards and scorecards, the Exco pack, budgets, the phone pass over every capture screen, help on every page, then a month of running beside PumpIT before the switch-off.

The plan behind this page holds 78 tasks in 20 work packages, each with what it delivers, how it is verified against real PumpIT data, and what it depends on. They are worked in that order, in parallel lanes where the dependencies allow. No calendar dates are promised on this page; the sequence is the commitment.

What happens to PumpIT. It keeps running throughout. When a branch's day is being closed in Agora and the numbers have agreed with PumpIT's for a full month, PumpIT's capture screens are switched off for that branch. Head-office reconciliation follows, reporting last. Nothing in the database is deleted in the first release, and the overnight imports are not touched.
